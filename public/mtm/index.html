Hackathon
Rules & FAQ

  • Each team must consist of 2 to 3 members enrolled in a recognized university in Pakistan. Maximum capacity is capped at 35 teams.
  • All registered members must be physically present for morning check-in (9:30 AM), project development, and afternoon jury demos.
  • All project prototypes, code, and architectures must be authored during the hackathon. Substantially pre-built projects or plagiarism will result in disqualification.
  • AI assistance and open-source frameworks are permitted for development, but the team must write, understand, and defend their core application logic.
  • Submissions are evaluated live on innovation, technical execution, practical problem solving, working demo quality, and presentation clarity.
